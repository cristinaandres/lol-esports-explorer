On a champion's By-era table, a row where the champion was eligible but never picked shows the same fact twice, two ways: `Games 204 | Picked — | Banned — | Presence 0% (0/204) | Won —`.

`ChampionDetail.tsx` renders Picked and Banned as `presenceOf(era) === 0 ? '—' : count(...)` while Presence is a `Ratio(part=0, total=era.games)` that prints "0% (0/204)". An em dash means "not a measurement" everywhere else on the site (`format.ts` says so); here 0 *is* the measurement, and the row contradicts itself.

171 zero-era rows exist corpus-wide. Master Yi is the case to look at: picked 3, banned 1 in pre-herald, then four post-release eras with 204/186/203/678 games and no pick.

Fix: render a measured zero as 0 (or `0% (0/204)` on all three columns, consistently), and reserve the dash for an era the champion could not be played in — the distinction the page already draws with `presenceOf`. Check both readings of the page against the data for a champion that is picked everywhere, one picked once, and one never released in time.
