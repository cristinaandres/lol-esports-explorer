Two reviewers reached this independently, with browser evidence: forcing a 503 on one eager file leaves seven routes at a bare `Could not load the dataset: …` with no retry and no advice — a dead end until a manual reload.

Routes: `GameDetail.tsx:45`, `EventDetail.tsx:52`, `EventsIndex.tsx:60`, `PlayerDetail.tsx:72`, `TeamDetail.tsx:51`, `ChampionDetail.tsx:43`, `Selection.tsx:55` (that last one for the selection artifact).

The same failure on `EraDetail`, `PhaseDetail` and `SeriesDetail` renders `LoadFailure` with a **Try again** that works; `Home` at least says to reload. `memoised` already forgets a rejection, so the capability exists — those seven routes just do not use it.

Check: force `eras.json` (and `selection.json`) to fail on each of the seven; the page must name the failure, offer a retry, and recover when the request is allowed. This is the pattern #20 established, so the fix should be small and consistent.

Found by the four-slice review of `main..dev` (slices 2 and 3).

failure was in three more — `ErasIndex`, `EntityIndex` and `Home` (Home told the reader to reload by
hand). Every route with a failed branch now renders `LoadFailure` with the loader's message and a
working retry; the only two route files without one are `NotFound`, which cannot fail, and `Glossary`,
