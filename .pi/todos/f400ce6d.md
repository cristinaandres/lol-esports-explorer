src/routes/ChampionDetail.tsx uses useGameDetail(), which fetches sides.json and picks.json (~236 KB gzip) to read playerGames and bans. That breaks the promise src/lib/loader.test.ts makes for career pages.

Add cached loadBans()/loadPicks() to src/lib/data.ts, compose loadGameDetail from them, and give the champion page the two it needs.

Check: the network tab on a champion page shows no sides.json and no picks.json.
