Both halves are done in 9a05dfe, and the ticket was right on four of the five names.

**Half 1, the dead exports.** `aliases.indexByName`, `format.fixed` and `phases.phasesOf` had no caller at all, so function and test went together. `career.careerFor` and `aliases.resolve` were test-only rather than unused, and neither justified keeping a production export for the tests: `careerFor`'s tests now compose `careerFrom(appearancesFor(...))`, the pair the player page composes, and the real-corpus alias assertions in `data.test.ts` now run through `search(buildSearchIndex(...))`, which is the lookup the search box uses. No assertion was dropped or weakened; the ones that moved now test live code. `Resolution` and the `indexByName` fixture rows went with their functions.

**Half 2, the win predicate.** `indexes.winnerKey` was kept and moved to a new `src/lib/game.ts`, because `indexes.ts` holds key-to-row maps and a game's result is not a lookup. It keeps the key shape (`winnerKey(game): number`) since titles, series tallies and game pages all need the team, not a boolean. Five hand-written copies were replaced: `series.ts`, `titles.ts`, `GameDetail.tsx` (series score) and `SeriesDetail.tsx` (tally and per-game line). The `row.side === 'blue' ? ...` family answers a different question and was left alone.

**Verified.** `npm run check` green (lint, 214 tests, `public/data is current`, production build). A throwaway harness wrote the three surfaces from the committed artifacts before and after — 3,091 game winners, 1,964 series tallies, 38 championships — and the files are byte-identical. `game.test.ts` keeps the part worth keeping: per-row equivalence plus the assumptions the predicate leans on (0/1 flag, two real and distinct team keys). On the built site in headless Chromium: `/series/mt-011d7924f3f7` shows 1–3 with both red-side games credited to Bilibili Gaming, `/games/gm-67a55b1a8098` shows the same scoreline and labels the red side the winner, and the BLG team page lists both titles as "3 – 1 in the final". The rendered title list itself was not opened page by page; its data was in the harness.

**Left alone.** The dated phase 3 specs still name `careerFor` in their "Test seams" prose. They record what was built on 2026-11-20, and rewriting them is not this ticket's job; a reader looking for the function should start from `src/lib/career.ts`.
