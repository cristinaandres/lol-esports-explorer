**Source:** adversarial review, findings 3 and 5.

**3. Coupled labels.** In `src/routes/EntityIndex.tsx`, the club abbreviation and the year are rendered inside one `entry.club ? … : null` branch, so a player whose last club has no `short_name` shows **neither** the club nor the year — losing the staleness signal that the year exists to provide (`Player.last_team_key`/`last_game_date` comments say so). No row is affected on this pin, so it is latent, not live.

**5. Stale order param.** `src/routes/EventsIndex.tsx` `setParam` deletes only the parameter it is setting, so switching `?sort=year&order=asc` back to Competition leaves `?order=asc` in the URL while the Order control disappears and `groupEvents` ignores it. A shared link then carries a parameter that does nothing.

**Fix.**
- Render the year independently of the abbreviation (club abbreviation when there is one, year always when there is a last game).
- When the sort becomes `competition`, delete `order` from the URL.

**Verify.** `/players` at 1440 and 390 px for a player whose club has a short and one that does not (synthetic fixture in a unit test is enough for the second); switching the events sort back to Competition leaves a bare `/events` URL.
