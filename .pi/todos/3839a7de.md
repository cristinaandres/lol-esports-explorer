**Source:** adversarial review, finding 2 (real, code-read).

**Two defects in `src/components/ClampedText.tsx`:**
1. The overflow measurement runs only on mount and when `children` change (deps `[expanded, children]`). There is no `ResizeObserver`, so: open a page wide (text fits, no button) then narrow the window and the note is ellipsised at two lines **with no "See more" at all**. The inverse leaves a button that reveals nothing — which the component's own docstring promises cannot happen.
2. The clamped paragraph keeps its content in the DOM, so the glossary `<Link>` inside the Distinctions note on `PlayerDetail` is **tab-focusable while invisible** (WCAG 2.4.7).

**Fix.**
- Measure with a `ResizeObserver` on the paragraph (and re-run on expand/collapse as today), so the control appears/disappears with the available width.
- While collapsed, keep the paragraph out of the tab order in a way that does not hide it from a screen reader's virtual cursor — e.g. `inert` on the clamped block with the toggle outside it, or render the link only when expanded and say so. Decide and document the choice in the component's docstring.
- Add a test for the measurement rule (a pure helper is easiest: given `scrollHeight`/`clientHeight`, does it overflow?), since jsdom cannot lay out.

**Verify.** In the browser: 1440 → 390 resize on `/players/<faker>` shows the toggle appearing; tab through the note collapsed and confirm focus does not land in hidden text; `npm run check`.
