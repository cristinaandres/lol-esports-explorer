# 07: Put the titles in the same place on both entity pages

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)
**Status:** ready

## What to build

On a team page the titles are the second thing after the figures; on a player page
they are the fifth, below the champion pool and the event list. Two pages of the same
kind, ordered differently for no reason a reader can infer.

Titles move to directly under the stat strip on both. They are the shortest, most
conclusive statement a page makes — "this is what this career amounts to" — and they
should not have to be found.

## Scope

- Section order on the player page only; the team page is already right.
- No content change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Titles appear immediately after the figures on both pages.
- [ ] No other section changes position.
- [ ] The navigation between the two pages reads the same way down.

## Validation

A browser check of the section headings on both pages, in order.
